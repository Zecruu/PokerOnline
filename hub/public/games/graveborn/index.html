Auto-fire bone bolts. Corpses are currency — you auto-raise them into a capped skeleton army. Level up and draft League-style cards. Class golds and prismatics reshape the grave.

Exit to Zecru Games

Combat

  • Move with WASD, or on a phone drag the MOVE stick (or anywhere on the field). Attacks and raises are automatic.
  • Killed enemies leave corpses. Every few seconds you raise the nearest corpse.
  • Army is capped. Extra raises explode the corpse so power never needs uncapped entities.
  • Waves scale every 20s. Elites glow gold. A named boss arrives about once a minute.

Cards

  • Silver = general stats. Gold / Prismatic = Necromancer upgrades.
  • Tap a card, or press 1 / 2 / 3 on a keyboard.
