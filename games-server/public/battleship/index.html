TACTICAL WATERS

Hot/cold bombs · Move your fleet each turn

How to play
  • Each turn: drop bombs on enemy waters (hits chain — keep firing until you miss) and move one ship by 1 cell at any time.
  • Bomb: the only attack. A miss reveals proximity — Burning / Warm / Cold / Freezing.
  • Move: shift one floating ship one cell orthogonally during your turn, before or after bombing.
  • Bombed zones: a cell that was bombed can't have a ship moved into it for 4 of your turns.
